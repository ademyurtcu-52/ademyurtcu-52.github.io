Coming soon on Google Play

A detective's case file has landed on your desk. Search the crime scene, collect clues, question the suspects and catch the killer!

Search the scene

Tap points of interest and find clues. Not everything is a clue; some things are there to mislead you.

Lab analysis

Send clues to Forensic Medicine, DNA, Toxicology, Ballistics and more. Get notified when results are ready; use boosters to shorten the wait.

Question suspects

Read statements, spot contradictions, match reports to suspects and weigh the polygraph notes.

Name the solution

Identify the killer, the method and the motive. You have limited attempts, so don't rush.

Rise and compare

Earn levels, ranks and badges, and measure yourself against friends and on the rankings.

Available in 11 languages. The game is free to play with optional in-app purchases and rewarded ads.
