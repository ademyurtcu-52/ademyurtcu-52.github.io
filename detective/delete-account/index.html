Delete your account

Detective: Lost File · Last updated: October 18, 2026

You can delete your account and the data linked to it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1: in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Detective: Lost File and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to the Profile screen.
  3. Tap Delete my account, enter your password and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: by e-mail

If you cannot open the game, send an e-mail to Yacsoft.play@gmail.com with the subject “Detective: Lost File account deletion” and write the e-mail address and nickname of the account. We will verify it and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • your account, e-mail address, nickname and sign-in data (password hash);
  • your progress: level, opened cases, clues, analyses, solutions and results, inventory, badges and ranks;
  • your friend list and friend requests, and your notification tokens.

What may be kept

  • anonymized records of purchase transactions, where accounting or tax law requires it;
  • short-lived technical server logs, which are removed automatically after a limited time.

Deleting an account cannot be undone. Items bought with real money are lost together with the account. Purchases made through Google Play can be managed in your Google Play account.
