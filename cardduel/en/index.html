Card Duel: Online Card Battle

Build decks, summon monsters and duel players online in this strategy card game.

Coming soon on Google Play

Build your deck, summon powerful monsters and outsmart your opponent! Card Duel is a strategic card duel game made for your phone.

🎮

How you play

Start with 8,000 life points and duel with monsters, spells and traps. Summon, attack, set traps and chain your cards to turn the tide of battle.

⚔️

Game modes

  • Story: fight through 8 chapters against ever tougher opponents
  • VS Computer: three difficulty levels, perfect for practice
  • Online Duel: ranked matches against real players, climb through the leagues
  • Friends: add friends with a friend code and challenge them to a duel
🃏

Collection & decks

  • Over 90 beautifully illustrated cards across three types: monsters, spells and traps
  • Open card packs and collect every rarity from Common to Legendary
  • Build and save multiple decks and find your best combo
🎁

Rewards

  • Daily login rewards, daily quests and achievements
  • A 30-tier season pass
  • Weekly and global leaderboards
🛡️

Fair & friendly

Preset emotes instead of free chat: no toxic behavior. Watch replays of your matches and learn from every duel.

Available in 11 languages. Card Duel is free to play with optional in-app purchases and optional rewarded video ads.
